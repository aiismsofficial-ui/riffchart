How Clone Hero uses these

On one song (Song details)

  • Highway: a still picture. Clone Hero can't animate a song's own highway.
  • Background: a picture, the music video, or an animated background (a video as long as the song).
  • Shown when Clone Hero's Song Highways / Use Song Backgrounds / Song Videos are on. They replace your everyday highway and background on that song.

Everyday, on every song (Install in Clone Hero)

  • Animated highway: in Clone Hero, on the difficulty screen press Start → Highway → “RiffChart - name”.
  • Animated background: Clone Hero Settings → Gameplay → Background Video.
  • They only show on songs that don't bring their own: for the animated highway on every song, turn off Song Highways (below). Each song keeps its own background (cover and name) unless you also turn off song backgrounds. Music videos still play.
