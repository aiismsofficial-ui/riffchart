    Welcome to RiffChart: three steps to your first chart

    1. Add a song. Drop an audio or music-video file in the box above (or paste a folder path). A minute or two later it's charted: guitar on all four difficulties, plus bass and keys when the song has them.
    2. Look it over. Press Edit next to the song and play it. Click to add a note, drag to move one, right-click to delete; anything unplayable is marked for you. Nothing changes until you Save.
    3. Play it. ▶ Clone Hero opens it in the game right away; Export copies it to your Clone Hero songs folder (then Scan Songs in Clone Hero).

    The charts are made by AI: handy for songs nobody has charted, or as a first draft to fix up. Found a spot it got wrong? Press F there in the editor; the Feedback file collects them for the developer.

    Tidy song names

    These names look like they came from a download or converter site. Untick anything that was right already. You can fix any song by hand later in its Song details.

    Re-chart
