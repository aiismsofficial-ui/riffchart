2 Where finished songs go

Export copies each finished song here. Clone Hero plays them from this folder.

3 Folders RiffChart never changes

Your own chart collections. RiffChart can open charts from them but will never save, export or delete anything inside them.

4 What Clone Hero shows while you play

RiffChart gives each song a background, a highway and (for music videos) a video. Clone Hero only shows them when these options are on.

Clone Hero has to be closed to change these (it saves its settings when it exits). Its settings file is backed up first.

5 Lyrics, bass and keys

Online, RiffChart looks the song up on lrclib.net, a free lyrics database (only the artist, title and length are sent), and times the words to the singing in your file. When it has none, or with "only on this PC", the singing is written down by speech recognition (Whisper) running on your PC: about half a minute a song with a graphics card, and a one-time 1.5 GB download. It mishears some words; fix them in the chart if you like. Clone Hero shows lyrics when its Lyrics option is on.

5-fret bass and keys parts (all four difficulties) from the song's bass and piano, which Clone Hero and YARG offer as its bass and keys instruments. The bass about doubles the time a song takes to chart. Keys add as much again on songs with a loud enough piano or keyboard, and are rougher than the guitar and bass charts (a busy first draft to fix up in the editor), so they're off unless you want them. Bring up to date adds them to songs charted before.

A song charted from a music video keeps its picture as the background video. Clone Hero only plays webm (VP8) videos on every platform, so mp4 and other videos are converted (under a minute a song). Bring up to date converts the videos of songs charted before.

6 Animated backgrounds

For animated backgrounds on single songs: Clone Hero plays a song's video once, so it has to last the whole song. Backgrounds and themes installed in Clone Hero loop, so they're always full sharpness and small.

7 Updates

The library shows a note with what's new. To know, RiffChart asks GitHub for its latest release (at most every 6 hours); nothing about you or your songs is sent.

Clone Hero's own options change only while it is closed.
