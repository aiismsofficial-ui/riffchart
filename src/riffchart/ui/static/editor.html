      Style for new takes
      Earlier versions

      Each save and re-chart keeps the version it replaced. Open one to look at it here; Save makes it the chart again (what you have now is kept too), Undo goes back.

        Tempo map

        Notes sit on beats, so they move with the tempo. Drag a beat line on the waveform to where the beat really is: the tempo before it stretches to fit and nothing after the next marker moves. Lock a marker (padlock) to keep it at its time when you change a tempo before it.

          Reported spots

          Places where the chart goes wrong, with your note. The library's Feedback file puts them all in one small file (song names, times, notes; no audio) you can send to the developer.

            Lyrics

            Change a line's words in its box (Enter). Drag a word on the waveform to where it's sung, or use Tap timing: play and press W as each yellow word starts. "Hel- lo" shows as "Hello" in the game. Save writes them into the chart.
